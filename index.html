At Mega Salez, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle, process, and protect information when you communicate with us through WhatsApp or any of our other communication channels.

Our goal is simple: Your privacy is our priority.

1. Information We Collect

Mega Salez may receive the following information when you communicate with us:

  • Name and contact information.
  • WhatsApp phone number.
  • Messages, queries, and information you voluntarily send to us.
  • Order-related information, if applicable.
  • Images, documents, or other files voluntarily shared by you.

We only access information that is necessary to communicate with customers and provide our services.

2. No Selling or Sharing of Customer Data

Mega Salez strictly respects customer confidentiality.

  • We do not sell customer information.
  • We do not rent or trade personal information.
  • We do not share customer information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • We do not disclose personal customer information to unauthorized individuals or organizations.
  • We do not use customer information for purposes unrelated to our services.

Your personal information is never a product for us to sell or trade.

Limited technical processing by WhatsApp, Meta, hosting providers, and authorized API infrastructure providers may occur where necessary to deliver our communication services.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Mega Salez follows a data-minimization approach.

  • We do not maintain an independent database or archive of customers' WhatsApp conversations or personal information.
  • We do not intentionally retain customer messages, images, or documents after the relevant communication has been processed.
  • We do not create customer profiles for unauthorized purposes.
  • We retain only information that is legally required or necessary to fulfill a legitimate business or legal obligation.

WhatsApp, Meta, and other infrastructure providers may independently process or temporarily retain certain information under their respective privacy policies and retention practices.

4. WhatsApp Business API Usage

Mega Salez may use the WhatsApp Business Platform or authorized WhatsApp API providers to communicate with customers.

These services may be used for:

  • Customer support.
  • Responding to customer inquiries.
  • Order-related notifications.
  • Sharing relevant product or service information.
  • Providing assistance requested by customers.

Your WhatsApp information is processed only as necessary for these communication purposes.

WhatsApp and Meta operate independently and have their own privacy policies and data practices.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer information against:

  • Unauthorized access.
  • Unauthorized disclosure.
  • Misuse of personal information.
  • Accidental loss or destruction.
  • Unauthorized modification.

We aim to use secure communication channels and restrict access to information to what is necessary for our operations.

However, no internet-based communication system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Customer Rights and Privacy

Customers have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we process.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information held by Mega Salez, subject to applicable legal requirements.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details provided below.

7. Cookies and Tracking
